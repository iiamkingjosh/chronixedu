# Who does what in Chronix Edu

For a school's staff, handed over at onboarding. Each role, what it is for, and where in the app it does it.
Every role sees only its own school, and only the screens of its own job: a screen another role uses is not in
your menu, and opening its address takes you back to your dashboard.

Written from the one table of who does what (8 Oct 2026). The app and this page read the same rules; if they
ever differ, the app is right and this page is out of date.

## Principal: runs the school

The principal is the school's administrator, and can also do the registrar's work and the bursar's, so a school
without a registrar or a bursar is never stuck.

- **Settings:** School Identity (name, logo, signature, stamp), Academic Structure (sessions and terms),
  School-wide Grading and Grading by Level (grade bands and pass mark), Assessment Config, Report Card
  Template, Notifications, Fee Settings, Payout Setup, Platform Billing and Data Export.
- **Staff accounts:** Settings → Users. Staff only: teachers, registrars, bursars and principals. A staff
  member's email address is changed by Chronix support, not in the school: it is the account's only key.
- **Classes and subjects:** Settings → Roster. **Who teaches what, and each class's form teacher,** are the
  principal's alone: a form teacher takes the class's attendance and writes its comments.
- **The timetable.**
- **Results:** approve, return or publish them (Academics → Results); report cards and principal's remarks.
- **Students and Promotions,** as the registrar does (below).
- **Fees,** as the bursar does (below): fee structures, invoices, payments, refunds and reminders.
- **School-wide announcements**, the attendance and behaviour overviews, attendance alerts, and analytics.

## Registrar: keeps the student records

- **Register students:** Students → Register Student, one at a time, or Bulk Import. Choose the student's
  class on the second screen. A student without a class this session is given one from their profile:
  **Assign Class**.
- **Correct a class** (with a reason), edit a student's details and photo, add a parent or guardian to a
  student, print transcripts.
- **Promotions** at the end of a session.
- **Classes and subjects:** Settings → Roster, the Classes and Subjects tabs. The form teacher and who teaches
  what are set by the principal.

## Bursar: keeps the money

- Fee structures, invoices, outstanding balances and collections.
- Records payments (cash, bank transfer, or a Paystack reference) and **refunds**.
- Fee reminders (to every parent with a balance, at most once an hour).
- **Looks students up** (Students), to find a child to invoice. Read only: the bursar does not register or
  change a student.
- Can **see** the school's payout account (Settings → Payout Setup) but not change it: only the principal can
  (decided 8 Oct 2026, "Only view to avoid changes"). Pays the school's Chronix subscription (Settings →
  Platform Billing).

## Teacher: teaches and assesses

- Score entry and result submission for the classes and subjects they are assigned this term.
- Attendance, behaviour, homework and class notices for the classes they teach or form-teach. A teacher sees
  the students of those classes, not the whole school's list.
- A form teacher also writes class comments.

## Parent and student: see their own

- **Parent:** their own children's published results, attendance, notices and messages, and fees, which they
  can pay online.
- **Student:** their own timetable, published results, homework and notices. Fees are for parents: a student
  sees no invoice and makes no payment.
- A parent account is created when a student is registered with a parent, or from the student's profile (Add
  Parent / Guardian). A student account is created by registering the student. Neither is made under Settings → Users,
  which is for staff.

## Setting up a new school, in order

1. The principal sets a password from the welcome email.
2. Settings: School-wide Grading (the grade bands and pass mark), then Assessment Config.
3. Settings → Roster: classes and subjects (the registrar can do this too).
4. Settings → Users: teachers, the registrar and the bursar.
5. Settings → Roster: which teacher takes which subject in which class this term, and each class's form teacher.
6. Registrar (or principal): register the students, each with a class.
7. Bursar (or principal): fee structures, then invoices. The principal sets up the payout account for online
   payment.
