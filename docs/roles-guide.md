# Who does what in Chronix Edu

For a school's staff, handed over at onboarding. Each role, what it is for, and where in the app it does it.
Every role sees only its own school. Written 8 Oct 2026, from what the app allows today.

## Principal: runs the school

The principal is the school's administrator, and can also do the registrar's work, so a school without a
registrar is never stuck.

- **Settings:** School Identity (name, logo, signature, stamp), Academic Structure (sessions and terms),
  School-wide Grading and Grading by Level (grade bands and pass mark), Assessment Config, Report Card
  Template, Notifications, Fee Settings, Payout Setup, Platform Billing and Data Export.
- **Staff accounts:** Settings → Users. Staff only: teachers, registrars, bursars and principals.
- **Classes, subjects and who teaches what:** Settings → Roster.
- **Results:** approve, return or publish them (Academics → Results); report cards and principal's remarks.
- **School-wide announcements**, attendance and behaviour overviews, analytics.
- Can view fees, but payments and refunds are recorded by the bursar.

## Registrar: keeps the student records

- **Register students:** Students → Register Student, one at a time, or Bulk Import. Choose the student's
  class on the second screen. A student without a class this session is given one from their profile:
  **Assign Class**.
- **Correct a class** (with a reason), edit a student's details and photo, add a parent or guardian to a
  student, print transcripts.
- **Promotions** at the end of a session.

## Bursar: keeps the money

- Fee structures, invoices, outstanding balances and collections.
- Records payments (cash, bank transfer, or a Paystack reference) and **refunds** (bursar only).
- Fee reminders, and the school's payout account (Settings → Payout Setup).

## Teacher: teaches and assesses

- Score entry and result submission for the classes and subjects they are assigned this term.
- Attendance, behaviour, assignments and class notices.
- A form teacher also writes class comments.

## Parent and student: see their own

- **Parent:** their own children's published results, attendance, notices and messages, and fees, which they
  can pay online.
- **Student:** their own timetable, published results, assignments and notices.
- A parent account is created when a student is registered with a parent, or from the student's profile (Add
  Parent / Guardian). A student account is created by registering the student. Neither is made under Settings → Users,
  which is for staff.

## Setting up a new school, in order

1. The principal sets a password from the welcome email.
2. Settings: School-wide Grading (the grade bands and pass mark), then Assessment Config.
3. Settings → Roster: classes and subjects.
4. Settings → Users: teachers, the registrar and the bursar.
5. Settings → Roster: which teacher takes which subject in which class this term.
6. Registrar (or principal): register the students, each with a class.
7. Bursar: fee structures, then invoices; the principal or bursar sets up the payout account for online payment.
